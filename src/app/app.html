@if(modal()==='about'){

About Iron Nest FCC

Minimal, unofficial player-built calculator for IRON NEST: Heavy Turret Simulator.

Elevation uses the community-tested in-game rule: distance (km) × 12 ÷ charges, with up to six powder charges.

Flight-time estimates use an experimental community model and should be checked in-game. Plot coordinates and axis directions remain provisional until verified against the official map.

Calculate projects the entered bearing and range from your Iron Nest coordinate onto the 20×10 km game grid. A missed shot can be corrected by entering either the updated target grid or the observed shell-impact grid. A vector correction updates bearing, distance and elevation while preserving the prior solution.

Firing cards, observations and routes are automatically saved to session-scoped IndexedDB. Reloading this tab keeps them; a new browser session starts empty and clears previous IndexedDB records. Browsers cannot guarantee immediate deletion when the last window closes. There is no server database or login.
